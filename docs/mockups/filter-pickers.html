Filter pickers

Each pill in the filter bar opens a panel attached to it, with a control made for that kind of value. A click on a row selects it alone, Shift- or Command-click adds it, the same as in the sidebar. Apply is live, so the list behind the panel updates while you pick; Esc or a click outside closes it.
